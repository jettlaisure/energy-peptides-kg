 01 — The Mark

## Amino chain

 Five hexagonal rings ascending left to right, joined by steel-blue bonds, with two side-chain stubs. The top-right ring is lit — it is the only element in the identity permitted to glow, and it is what makes the mark read as energy rather than as generic chemistry. The ascent and the lit terminal ring are both load-bearing: never level the chain, never move the light.

 Clear space

 Keep clear on all four sides by 1× , where × is the height of a single hexagon ring. Nothing — type, rule, photo edge, or button — enters that field.

 Minimum size

 Mark alone 24 px 8 mm 

 Horizontal lockup 140 px 32 mm 

 Stacked lockup 96 px 22 mm 

 Widths for the lockups, height for the mark. Below 24 px drop the inner ring bonds — they fill in and the mark turns to mud on a vial cap or a browser tab.

 Do

 Place the mark on #0D1B3E or darker. It is a dark-ground identity.

 Use the gold gradient, not a flat fill, at any size above 24 px.

 Reproduce the lit ring at full brightness, or drop the glow entirely for one-colour print.

 On photography, sit the lockup on a solid navy plate rather than directly on the image.

 Don't

 Rotate, mirror, arch, or level the chain — the ascent is the mark.

 Move the light to another ring, or add a second glowing ring.

 Substitute flat yellow ( #FFD700 and similar) for the brushed gold.

 Outline, emboss, or drop-shadow the wordmark.

 Set the lockup on white without redrawing it in the light-surface palette.

 Rebuild the chain with a different ring count.

 02 — Colour

## Navy, gold, steel

 Deep navy is the ground and carries roughly 80% of any surface. Gold is the single accent — one gold element per view, load-bearing. Steel blue is the connective tissue: bonds, eyebrows, secondary type. Periwinkle is drawn from the lyophilised powder itself and is reserved for data and spec values.

 03 — Typography

## Satoshi, five weights

 Satoshi by Indian Type Foundry, via fontshare.com . One family does the whole system — hierarchy comes from weight and letter-spacing, never from a second face. Self-host the WOFF2 web kit rather than hotlinking; confirm the current licence terms on Fontshare before launch. Fallback stack: "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif .

 Retatrutide Black · 900 · display & wordmark 

 Tesamorelin Bold · 700 · headings, product names 

 Lyophilised Powder Medium · 500 · labels, buttons, eyebrows 

 10 mL vial, sealed Regular · 400 · body copy 

 Research use only Light · 300 · lede, 20 px and above 

 Light 300 below 20 px disappears against navy — it is a display weight here, not a body weight. Satoshi also ships italics and a 300–900 variable file; the variable is the better web payload if the build supports it.

 Grid & rhythm

 Content max 1200 px , 12 columns, 24 px gutters. Page gutter 32 px desktop / 20 px mobile. Long-form measure caps at 720 px inside the 1200. Section rhythm 128 px desktop, 64 px mobile. Everything lands on an 8 px baseline.

 Form & elevation

 Radii stay small — 2 px on controls, 4 px on panels, full pill only on status chips. No soft drop shadows anywhere; separation comes from hairlines and a one-step navy lift. The only permitted glow is gold, on the lit ring and on primary-button hover.

Powder reads periwinkle #A8B4DC under this light — keep it, it is a brand cue. Product shots are square (1:1) for grids and 4:5 for editorial. Never composite a vial onto white.

 05 — Copy Rules

## Research-use-only language

 This is the constraint that shapes the site more than any colour choice. Every product page, meta description, alt text, and button label is written so nothing on the site states or implies a human use, an effect, or an outcome. Design decisions that create a slot for effect copy — a "Benefits" tab, a "Shop by goal" filter, a testimonial block — should not be built.

 Don't ship Ship instead Why

 Required on every product view

 For Research Use Only — Not for Human Consumption

 Compliance line at 10 px / +0.16em, Gold Highlight on navy or Brand Navy on paper. It sits inside the product panel, above the fold, not in the footer. Same wording everywhere — no shortened variants.

 Open before launch

 COA links are blank on all 22 SKUs, and purity is blank on all 22. The COA is what the whole research-use position rests on — a product page with a compliance line and no document behind it is weaker than no page at all. Design the product template with the COA link as a required, prominent element so the gap is visible while it is being filled.
