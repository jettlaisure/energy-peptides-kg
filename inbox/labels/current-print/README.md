## Choose one print route
1. Process: use the main PDF in each colour folder. Gold is simulated CMYK.
2. Metallic foil: use the paired foil/*_underprint.pdf plus foil/*_foil.pdf. The latter has the named FOIL_GOLD separation for the border, wordmark, dose, disclaimer and ring faces. Do not also print the process master. White cuts on the separation represent openings, not a white ink plate. Printer must set trapping, registration and knockout/overprint for its process.

Colour is generic DeviceCMYK converted from approved RGB values, not printer-profiled PDF/X. The converter must supply its output condition and confirm gold/field colours on stock. Navy #0D1B39–#15294C, Graphite #2B2F34, Black #0B0B0C; steel-blue bonds #6E8CB8.

Use fine textured matte synthetic stock, freezer-rated permanent adhesive suitable for the actual vial, without gloss laminate. Compliance and vial-size copy are 3.49 pt on standard labels (about 0.88 mm cap height); wordmark 4.07 pt. These small sizes preserve the approved design and need an actual-stock legibility proof. Smallest ring outlines are about 0.018 mm and interior/bond strokes about 0.15 mm; the foil converter must assess fine detail reproduction. Obtain a physical print/wrap/freeze/thaw proof before bulk production. This package is dimensioned for printer handoff, not a certification of printer-specific colour, foil or stock performance.
